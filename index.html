04 · AI Security

AI is powerful. It also needs boundaries.

Building generative systems is only half the job — the other half is knowing what to block, flag, or let through.

🛡 Built at Entrans

Aegis AI Gateway

An AI security and data-firewall layer that inspects content before it reaches — or leaves — an AI system. Every input is screened for malware, sensitive data, and harmful content, then routed to a policy decision.

It reflects an approach to AI engineering that treats safety as infrastructure: validation, detection, and policy enforcement built directly into the pipeline, not bolted on afterward.
